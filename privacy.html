Privacy, in plain language.

This notice describes the current static website preview. It is not a substitute for the final privacy notice that must be published after a server-side form, analytics, or CRM is connected.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What this preview stores

If you submit the project enquiry form, the name, email address, project type, package recommendation, assessment reference and message are saved in this browser’s local storage. The six-question assessment itself does not send answers to a server. Required consent is requested before the form is submitted.

The chosen theme and dismissal of the privacy notice may also be saved in this browser. The static preview does not use advertising cookies, analytics or a server-side lead database.

Who receives the information?

In this preview, no enquiry is transmitted to Subhadip Digital Solutions or stored on a server. Clearing this browser’s site data removes the locally saved enquiry. If you choose to use a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp receives information you choose to send, under its own privacy terms.

Do not submit sensitive personal, financial or health information through this preview form.

Purpose, retention and your choices

The form asks for only basic contact and project information so the business can respond to your enquiry once a secure form service is configured. Local preview entries remain in the browser until its site data is cleared. You can avoid submitting the form or clear local site data at any time.

For questions about an enquiry shared by WhatsApp, contact Subhadip Digital Solutions at +91 85850 04555 or WhatsApp.

Before the production launch

The live operator must connect and name the form/CRM provider, explain where and how long submissions are retained, provide a deletion/contact process, and update this notice before accepting server-side enquiries. Any analytics should be enabled only with appropriate notice and consent, with conversion events configured for the chosen analytics property.
