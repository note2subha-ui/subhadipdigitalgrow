06 / Good questions

Curious?
Start here.

Still have a question? I’m always happy to talk it through.

Ask me directly ↗
How much does a website cost?

Packages start at ₹4,999. Your written quote depends on page count, features, content, deadline and any add-ons such as domain or hosting.

How soon can my website go live?

A typical small-business website takes about 7–14 days once the scope and content are ready. Larger stores or custom features may take longer.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Your website and supplied content are yours. Domain and hosting arrangements are discussed clearly in the quote.

Is hosting included in the package price?

Hosting and domain requirements are confirmed separately in your written quote, so there are no hidden assumptions.

Can I request changes after launch?

Yes. You can request changes after launch, or choose the ₹999/month maintenance starting plan for ongoing care. The exact support scope is agreed first.

Do you work in Hooghly and Kolkata?

Yes. DigitalGrow is based in Hooghly, West Bengal and serves local businesses in Hooghly, Kolkata and nearby areas.
